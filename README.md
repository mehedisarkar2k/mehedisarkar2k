# Mehedi Hasan Sarkar

Product engineer for web and mobile. I build booking, payments and learning products. I add AI features that users can trust.

For 5 years I have worked with clients in Australia, Sweden and the UK. My case studies show the problem, the decision and the trade-off: https://www.mehedisarkar.com/work?ref=github

## What I build

- **Booking.** A 10-minute slot hold, so two people cannot book the same court at the same moment. Reminders, cancellations and club payouts. [Case study](https://www.mehedisarkar.com/work/booking-race?ref=github)
- **Payments.** Stripe in 4 products: manual capture, Connect payouts, refunds and 3D Secure. Money moves only when a booking is really going ahead. [Case study](https://www.mehedisarkar.com/work/payments-hold-first?ref=github)
- **Inherited systems.** I took over a live salon booking system. I fixed the 31 defects an audit had listed and cut the mobile payload from 17.2 MB to 1.7 MB. [Case study](https://www.mehedisarkar.com/work/inherited-booking-system?ref=github)
- **Learning apps and AI.** A Gemini OCR pipeline turns scanned textbooks into structured content. It has page timeouts, retries and recovery after a worker crash. [Case study](https://www.mehedisarkar.com/work/textbook-pipeline?ref=github)
- **Mobile apps.** I led a mobile team of 3 developers on 2 products and owned the App Store and Google Play releases. I worked on a padel booking app with 100K+ downloads on Google Play.

## Stack

TypeScript, React, Next.js, React Native, Expo, Node.js, Express, NestJS, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Redis, Stripe, Docker, GitHub Actions, Vercel.
