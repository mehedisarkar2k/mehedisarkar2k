# Mehedi Hasan Sarkar

Full-Stack Engineer, web and mobile. For 4+ years I have built booking, payment and learning products for clients in Australia, Sweden and the UK.

I start with what can go wrong before I build a feature. The case studies show the problem, the decision and the trade-off: https://www.mehedisarkar.com/work?ref=github

## What I work on

- **Booking platforms.** A 10-minute slot hold, so two people cannot book the same court at the same moment. Reminders, cancellations and club payouts. [Case study](https://www.mehedisarkar.com/work/booking-race?ref=github)
- **Payments.** Stripe across 4 products: manual capture, Connect payouts, refunds and 3D Secure. Money only moves when the booking is really going ahead. [Case study](https://www.mehedisarkar.com/work/payments-hold-first?ref=github)
- **Inherited systems.** On one salon booking system I documented 31 defects, fixed them in priority order and cut the mobile payload from 17.2 MB to 1.7 MB. [Case study](https://www.mehedisarkar.com/work/inherited-booking-system?ref=github)
- **AI content pipelines.** Gemini OCR that turns scanned textbooks into typed content blocks, with per-page timeouts, retries and recovery after a worker crash. [Case study](https://www.mehedisarkar.com/work/textbook-pipeline?ref=github)
- **Mobile apps.** Led a mobile team of 3 developers on 2 products and owned the App Store and Google Play releases. Worked on a padel booking app with 100K+ Google Play downloads.
